02 / A SECOND INVARIANCE

The same principle discovers orientation.

Here the input is not stereo. It is a 600 × 600 image of randomly sized, smoothly oriented ellipses. A different 26–9–1 network learns on 5 × 5 image patches over a 120 × 120 output lattice.

03 / REPLICATION & LIMITS

Evidence, not just a promising screenshot.

Three independently initialized runs of each experiment are measured automatically on CI. The site shows every seed, the mean and standard deviation, and the paper's reported values separately.

Stereo generalization across seeds

Absolute Pearson correlation |r| on the Gaussian training surface and independently generated egg-box test pattern.

1997 paper: reported sample |r| above 0.92 training, above 0.89 unseen; ten-run means 0.905 training / 0.869 test. The figures here are new measurements, not those values.

Orientation generalization across seeds

Fraction of output variance explained by a periodic orientation fit, trained as a diagnostic on training patches and applied unchanged to unseen patches.

The 1997 paper reported mean F ≈ 0.903 across 15 orientation runs. Our F and R² are not the same statistic; the original data, random draws and optimizer differ.

LEARNING RULE

Spatial invariance

Maximise F = log((V + ε)/(U + ε)) where U measures local output changes and V measures deviation from the global mean. A toroidal exponential averaging mask has half-life 2 for stereo and 1 for orientation. Gradients are analytic, checked against finite differences, and propagated through tanh units.

GENERALIZATION

No reuse of training stimuli

The stereo test uses a separate seeded random-dot texture and a different egg-box disparity field. The orientation test uses different random ellipse lengths and texture realization. Test signals are used for scoring and visualization only.

REPLICATION FIDELITY

Differences are disclosed

Network scale and core learning objective follow the paper, but our high-resolution stimulus synthesis, exact egg-box parametrization, random draws, initial weight distribution and SciPy conjugate-gradient optimizer are reconstructions, not byte-for-byte reproductions of the original experimental program.
